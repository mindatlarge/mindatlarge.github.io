High Pedigree, Agency

Task: Identity
Year: 2025



The identity for High Pedigree is centred around a simple geometric mark that merges an arrow with the letter “I” from the name. Its upward movement suggests growth and progression, translating the agency’s strategic role into a clear and adaptable visual device.

Tunnel, Electronic Club

Task: Event Visual Identity
Year: 2023-2025



For several seasons, Club Tunnel’s weekly electronic music programme required a flexible visual system across flyers, posters and social media. To simplify the design process and handle the volume of weekly events, a modular information block was developed and multiplied through repetition to fill different formats, creating strong graphic compositions while keeping all event information clear and visible.

Museum of Contemporary Art Belgrade

Task: Catalogue Design
Year: 2024



Catalogue design for Jelena Bulajić’s exhibition at the Museum of Contemporary Art in Belgrade, produced in a 130 × 180 mm format. Following the artist’s request for a classical, almost undesigned appearance, the book relies on restrained typography, structure and proportion rather than overt graphic intervention.

Zdrava Navika, Honey

Task: Packaging Design
Photo: Miljan Vuletic
Year: 2023



Zdrava Navika is a local producer of high-quality organic honey. The task was to create a distinctive packaging design that would set the brand apart from other producers while emphasising the authentic, homemade character of its non-industrial honey.

Decode, Architectural Studio

Task: Identity
Creative Direction: Peter Gregson Studio
Year: 2023



The identity for the architecture studio Decode is built through a custom typographic system combining sharp 90-degree angles with softer curves, creating forms reminiscent of brutalist architecture and its geometric structures. Each character of the name can also function independently as a graphic element, extending the identity beyond the logo into a flexible visual system.

Cubismo, Vape

Task: Packaging Design
Year: 2022



The challenge was to develop a packaging system for ten different vape flavours while making each variant immediately recognisable at the point of sale. Each flavour was assigned a distinct background colour, creating a strong visual range, while the proportions and construction of the packs subtly reference the familiar format of a cigarette pack.

Stool

Task: Product Design
Photo: Jelena Bulajic
Year: 2022



The design brief required the stool to be formed from a single sheet of material. The opening and radius were shaped to improve grip and handling while simultaneously defining the overall form. The prototype shown in the images was produced in aluminium.
