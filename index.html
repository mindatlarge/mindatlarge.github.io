Nemanja Karisic is designer and art director working across branding, visual identities, packaging, editorial and digital design. His experience spans projects of different scales and industries. He believes that meaningful design starts with purpose and function — where form follows the idea behind it.

Sova Brewery came to the project with an existing logo and wanted its three beers to move away from the expected visual language of beer towards a more refined, wine-inspired character. Each name became the starting point for a minimal symbol: Bobot is represented by a mountain peak, Hedvig by the two eyes of an owl, while Amber uses an inverted triangle to suggest depth, richness and the darker character of the red beer.

Client: Sova Brewery
Task: Packaging Design
Year: 2023
